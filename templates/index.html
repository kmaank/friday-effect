What is the Friday Effect?

The Friday Effect (also called the "weekend effect") is the idea that stock prices tend to be lower on Fridays than on other days. The theory: investors sell holdings before the weekend to avoid holding risk over two non-trading days, pushing prices down. But is this actually true? This dashboard tests that claim using real market data from Yahoo Finance. Pick any index and date range above, then hit Analyze.
